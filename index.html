About the book

Teenager v The System follows four complaints I took on myself as a teenager, against a district council, a bank, a town council and a county council.

A Land Charges outage at the district council was holding up my family’s house move. Complaining got the searches moving faster. The bank gave me wrong information about rewards eligibility, so I took it to the Financial Ombudsman. The town council never gave me the win I was after. The county council dispute is still open, and so is the Ombudsman’s decision. Both are saved for book two.

The last chapter covers how I go about a complaint, start to finish.
